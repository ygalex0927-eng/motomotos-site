07 — Questions

Good questions.

What is motomotos?

motomotos is a Dynamic Island app for Mac. It turns the MacBook notch into a black island that changes shape, like the Dynamic Island on iPhone. Hover for a glance, click to open a toolbox: music, calendar, a pomodoro timer, battery, your apps and more — 17 widgets — plus the status, quota left and approval alerts of AI coding agents such as Claude Code, Codex and Grok.

How much does it cost?

10 days free with every feature. Then $4.99 once — no subscription, future Mac updates included, on up to three of your Macs.

Which Macs does it run on?

Apple silicon Macs on macOS 15 or later. It looks best on a MacBook with a notch; with the lid closed on an external display, or on a Mac without a notch, the island sits at the top middle of the menu bar.

Which AI tools does it work with?

Claude Code, Codex, Grok, DeepSeek Harness, Hermes and WorkBuddy — desktop apps and terminals, one switch each. For anything else, or a long build, the motomotos notify command lights the island too.

Where does the quota come from?

Grok and Codex from their own records; Claude Code’s quota arrives once you have used claude in a terminal (the quota card has an “Open in Terminal” button). Unreadable means unreadable — never an estimate.

What happens to my data?

motomotos runs on your Mac. It doesn’t scrape your keychain, collect credentials or upload your code or chats. The privacy policy has the details.

What happens after 10 days?

The AI agent features (sessions, approvals, alerts, quota, tokens) and the other widgets lock until you buy. The island, the clock and the cat keep working.

How many Macs can I use?

Up to three of your own Macs at once. On another Mac, open Restore Purchase in the app and enter the license key from your receipt email.

Is it notarized by Apple?

Yes. Since 1.1.0, Motomotos.dmg from this site is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple: open it, drag Motomotos into Applications, done — no “can’t be verified” warning.
