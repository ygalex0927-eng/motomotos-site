Changelog

What changed in each version of motomotos. To update, use Settings › General › Check for Updates in the app, or download the latest version any time.

Updated November 19, 2026

1.1.2 November 20, 2026

  • Buy inside the app. A new purchase window: pay with Creem and this Mac unlocks on its own. Waiting, success, timeout and cancel each have their own screen. Activation codes are gone; a license key from your receipt email goes under Restore Purchase.
  • Clear trial rules. After the 10-day trial the AI agent features and the other widgets lock until you buy; the island, the clock and the cat keep working. A paid Mac no longer locks when the license server can’t be reached.
  • Feels local. Dates and times follow your Mac’s region, 12/24-hour setting and time zone; new installs pick °F / mph from your measurement system; permission prompts are translated into all six languages; update notices no longer pop up a dialog at launch.
  • The island closes when you leave it by default, and only one copy of motomotos can run at a time.
  • Lighter and steadier. Less CPU when idle, faster drawing while the island opens, a music helper that checks less often and quits with the app, and Reminders sync that never deletes your local to-dos when it can’t read the list.

1.1.0 November 13, 2026

  • Redesigned settings, an improved island layout, background previews, and smoother opening and closing.
  • Notarized by Apple. Motomotos.dmg is signed with a Developer ID and notarized: open it and drag Motomotos into Applications, with no “can’t be verified” warning.

1.0.5 October 28, 2026

  • Pay and it unlocks — no key. Settings › Account › Buy for $4.99: pay in the browser and this Mac unlocks on its own a moment later. Keys bought on the website before still work, on up to 3 Macs.
  • The Buy button on motomotos.com opens the app to buy, so paying on the site unlocks your Mac the same way.

1.0.4 October 27, 2026

  • Redesigned island. Springy opening and closing; the rim glows with what’s going on. Done and error alerts redrawn: a tick that draws itself with sparks, a shake of the head on errors.
  • Hover band. Rest on the notch for running sessions, AI usage left, now playing, the clock and the focus timer; click to open.
  • Music in the notch. Cover art and an equalizer; while an agent works and a song plays, the island pinches off a small drop for the music.
  • More AI agents: Claude Code, Codex, Grok, DeepSeek Harness, Hermes and WorkBuddy, desktop apps and terminals, one switch each.
  • AI usage left for every agent, with the exact reset day and time.
  • Pomodoro with an alert in the notch when time is up.
  • Requires macOS 15 or later. After updating, turn Screen Recording and Automation back on for motomotos in System Settings › Privacy & Security.

1.0.3 October 25, 2026

  • Activation codes bought on the website can be redeemed in Settings › Account, on up to 3 Macs.

1.0.2 October 25, 2026

  • Claude Code alerts. One click in Terminals & Approval, and the island lights up when Claude Code finishes, fails or waits for you.
  • Alerts from any tool: motomotos notify, motomotos run -- <command> and motomotos://notify links.
  • Agents connected on first launch: the welcome steps find Grok CLI, Claude Code, Codex and Hermes and send a test alert.

1.0.1 October 24, 2026

  • Session status and approvals work on a fresh install.
  • No more approving twice: Follow terminal (default) or Approve in the notch.
  • The island shows with the lid closed on an external display, and on Macs without a notch.
  • The 10-day trial, then a $4.99 license that keeps every feature.
